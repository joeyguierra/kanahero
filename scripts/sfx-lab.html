Sound Lab

Everything in sfx-src/, tuned by ear. Move the cap, the target peak and the head trim, hear it, then SAVE writes sfx-src/sfx.config.json and BAKE runs the real ffmpeg pass into public/sfx/. The browser decides; ffmpeg renders — so after a bake you can play the actual .m4a and hear what AAC did to it.

The slice can start anywhere in the file. Drag on the waveform to place the head, drag its right edge to set the cap, double-click to hear the raw take from that point. A multi-take recording shows every onset in green — ◀ HIT / HIT ▶ jumps between them, so the fourth click in a four-click take is one button away.
