PRINTED · RESTS

Once per open, first card only. The legend is visible throughout. Reduced motion: printed at once. A card with no receipt opens printed. E2E with motion on: ink face inside 400 ms, printed after 1 s.

THE HELD FACE

Chip stays. Mid and meaning go. The body under the chip becomes bone paper, drawInk at scale = 180 / box.w, letterboxed, never cropped. The model word sits over it as WordReveal animate=false, cells at 180 / N — S7b at half scale. The foot's kind line reads ATTEMPT n. The face says when, the ink says how.

THE HOLD

150 ms on pointerdown; release restores the face at once, no toggle to get stuck in. The strokes play in recorded time, sped to fit 2 s; held past the end they rest finished. Enter / Space toggles for keyboard and switch users — that is what e2e drives. Tap outside closes; tap on the card is inert.

FLAGS — YOUR CALL

Model over ink. S7b prints the model at .9 strike over faded ink. Here the ink is the subject, so I inverted it: ink full, model at .55 (tweakable). §2.6 says the panel must match the canvas — if that means opacity too, this flips back.

Paper edge. The bone body has no border — the card's own stock is the frame. On a base card (plate on plate) it still reads because the paper is bone.

THE TRACK

Horizontal scroll-snap, one 214 card per snap, 36 px gap. Centred, the neighbours peek 52 px at each edge — that peek is the swipe affordance, so the arrows can stay quiet. touch-action: pan-x: a moving finger gets the browser's pan and cancels the hold; a still one gets the hold.

THE PAGER

‹ 1 / 3 › in the detail-position style under the legend. Arrows are 44 px targets, bone when they step, carbon at the end of the track; the number is not tappable. Hidden entirely at one card.

THE CHIP

One suffix prop, three readings. S8: SHINY · 1st TRY. Receipted: SHINY · 9-18-26, no leading zeros. Legacy: SHINY · ×2, the copies without a receipt. Tries move to the foot on hold, so nothing is lost when the date takes the chip.

THE LEGACY CARD

Always last. No hold, no legend — 1f keeps the legend's row height so the pager does not jump when you swipe onto it. 1g is every stock in the app the day this ships: one card, no pager, and the Joker's one new line on the screen beneath. The screen cannot tell a pre-v6 copy from a failed write and does not try.

FLAGS — YOUR CALL

The peek. Spec says one card per snap; it does not say the neighbours show. I think they should. Toggle it off in tweaks to see the alternative.

Legend row on the legacy card. Reserved (blank) vs collapsed. Blank wins on a track; collapse if you would rather the lone card in 1g and the tracked one match.

Two runs, one day. Two cards read SHINY · 9-25-26 and are told apart only by ink and tries. Spec accepts that; flagging it so it is a decision.
