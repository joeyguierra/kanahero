THE MELT

One SVG filter on the card: fractal noise → displacement → blur. The whole card is present from the first frame but pushed around by the noise — edges run, the kana smears — and over the duration the displacement drains to zero and the blur sharpens. Opacity reaches full by 40%; scale settles from 0.92 to 1 with a small drop from 8px above. Ease-out cubic.

THE SMOKE

Off by default. Optional strike-pink radial behind the card that flares to peak at 30% and is gone by the end. The Joker tips −6° toward the card as it starts and recovers as it hardens. Nothing else on the screen moves; the canvas is pinned.

HOOK

Fires when the prompt card mounts in S7 — after the deal, and again on each next hand. Budget ≈ 900ms; the player can start drawing at any point, the melt does not block input. Reduced motion: 160ms fade, no filter.
