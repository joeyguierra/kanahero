FLAGS — YOUR CALL

Colors. Kept as proposed: pre-check blueprint, drill live, night caution. Live for the drill is right — it is the only part the app measures itself. I would swap the other two only if night should read as the quieter part; caution is the loudest color on the page and night is the newest column.

Untimed mark. Hatched tick, carbon on carbon, under the date (tweak: untimedMark = dash swaps it for —). The hatch echoes S1's due cards and the legend explains it once; the dash matches the table. I lean hatch.

9 vs 30 days. The chart plate is fluid; columns shrink to a 28 px floor (about 24 days at this width), after which the plate scrolls horizontally, newest in view, oldest off the left. No compression into weeks — a week total is an average in disguise.

Table vs chart order. Chart above, table below, as drawn: the chart answers "what did the week look like", the table is for checking against results.md column by column. On the phone the chart still comes first.

NIGHT DRILL 0.0. Drawn as briefed, but night 1 is untimed and night 2 has not happened — nothing measured sums to 0.0, so the chip is close to counting an untimed part as zero. 4 shows — for a part with no measured value; I would use that in 1 too.

NOT YET. Tonight's night sheet reads NOT YET in carbon mono and no tick, since it is neither untimed nor absent. Drop the words and leave the cell empty if that is one word too many on a screen that never comments.

Sheet, not drawer. The brief says bottom sheet; on desktop a bottom sheet over a 1280 canvas floats oddly, so it is docked right at phone width. On the phone it becomes the bottom sheet unchanged.

1b's date label. "No label" read as no words about absence; the date stays so the axis reads. Remove it if you want the gap truly blank.
