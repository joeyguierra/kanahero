THE ROW

Cards are {{ cardW }}×{{ cardH }}. Rows bleed to the phone edge with a 10px gutter, so a full row of seven has {{ overlap }}px of overlap per card. Dealt left to right, so the rightmost card is on top of the stack. A short row keeps the same overlap and left-aligns — it does not spread.

ONE PIECE

Nothing inside a card moves to dodge the overlap. Chip top-left, romaji bottom-left, kanji centered — identical on every card. The card on top hides the right {{ overlap }}px of the one beneath; tapping a card opens its full face over the screen — stock and tries, the set's mark, the word, its reading and what it means — which is where a card is read. The shelf on S6d opens the same face, counting copies instead of tries.

THE REVEAL

Two beats. Deal — the Joker flicks all ten face down from his hand, left to right, 80ms apart, same arc as S6b; the count reads 0. Turn — worn first, then base, then shiny, each a 240ms Y-flip in place 160ms apart, the count and tally ticking up per card. Before each shiny he holds 450ms, the card lifts 6px and flips slower (320ms) with a pink flare. He speaks and the note appears when the last card is down. Once a shiny is face up, a diagonal white swipe crosses it in 700ms, every 3s, the two shinies offset so they never swipe together.

HOOK

Budget ≈ 1.2s deal + 2.4s turn at defaults. In the app a tap through the reveal hurries the rest of the hand over rather than cutting it — 140ms flips, 70ms apart, no shiny holds — and a second tap snaps what is left flat. On this sheet, tap any card or the Joker to replay. Reduced motion: all faces up, counts final, one 160ms fade.
