V5D SOUND INVENTORY reviewed against v5c handoff · e3cd9e3 · 2026-09-19

Every sound the app needs, in the order it needs them

SPEC-v5d §1 names seven files, all on the reveal and the tick. Reviewing all ten screens gives 28 cues in 21 more files, five of them reused at a second playback rate. Everything below inherits §0 without exception: default off, nothing sound-only, generate at 0.5 s and trim, anechoic, AAC. Foundation is the old-RPG vocabulary — text blip, cursor, confirm, cancel, item-get, fanfare — every one of it re-voiced as card-table foley. No chimes, no melody, one ring.

TIER 1The loop — fires every word, 9–21× a run. These are what the app sounds like.8 cues · 7 files
ink.looppointer held and moving on the canvas · Round.tsx2 s SEAMLESS LOOP · −26 → −20 dBFS · 1 file

Pencil on paper. The one continuous sound in the app and the one that is heard most — this is where the ASMR lives. Gain and playbackRate follow pointer speed (silent at rest, full at ~600 px/s), 8 ms fades on down and up. The only cue you cannot trim into being: generate 2 s, find a seamless cut, crossfade. No old-school ancestor — nothing in 1990 had a pen.

ink.downpointerdown on the canvas≤40 ms · −22 dBFS · RR3 ±3%

The tip touching paper — a single soft graphite tap, the attack the loop lacks. ink.up (the lift, ≤30 ms, −26) is the same generation trimmed from the other end; build it, then A/B against the loop's fade alone and cut it if the fade is enough.

ink.revealFLIP · Round.tsx write→reveal · his model over your ink≤250 ms · −16 dBFS · 1 file (RR3 if M12 animates strokes)

One brush stroke on paper — brush, not pencil. The answer is the Joker's and it must not sound like yours. Today FLIP is a one-frame cut (M12 open), so one swish covers it; if the model ever draws itself stroke by stroke, one per stroke, round-robin.

joker.talkM1 type-on, 22 ms/char · Joker.tsx · every screen≤25 ms · −24 dBFS · RR4 ±6%

The RPG text blip, and the foundation of the whole palette — but not one per character. Forty-five characters at 22 ms is a rattle, the same law as §0.2. One dry paper click every third character, skipping spaces and punctuation, four variants cycled with pitch jitter. Paper, not electronics: a typewriter platen heard from the next room, not a beep. Silent whenever the panel is empty — S1 before storage lands, S8 until the last card. Japanese and Latin get the same voice; two voices would be cute and excessive.

hand.missMISSED · Round.tsx missed() · the word goes back with a gap of 2≤180 ms · −16 dBFS · 1 file

One card slid back under a deck on felt: dull, no snap, no ring. The exact opposite gesture to hand.tick, which is what M9 is missing visually too. Old-school "damage", gentled to nothing — never a buzzer, never a minor interval. His line is already kind; the sound must not undercut it.

ink.clearCLEAR · the canvas wiped≤300 ms · −18 dBFS · 1 file

A hand brushing eraser dust off paper, once. ink.undo is the same file at rate 1.4 trimmed to ≤120 ms and −20 — one stroke lifted, tighter, smaller. One generation, two cues.

TIER 2Deals and arrivals — once per run, the beats between screens5 cues · 5 files
deal.pressDEAL · S6b CTA · the run begins≤400 ms · −12 dBFS · 1 file

A deck squared and cut once. The old-school "level start", and the bookend to reveal.end — the same deck heard opening and closing a run. Generate at 1 s like reveal.end. The most important single non-loop sound outside S8.

prompt.meltM5 · every presentation, 900 ms · PromptMelt.tsx≤700 ms · −24 dBFS · 1 file

The card being made: paper fibre settling, grain draining to silence. The one texture in the app and the closest thing to ambience — but it fires 9–21 times a run, so it must be felt, not heard. Ducks to nothing while ink.loop is active. If in testing it reads as noise, the answer is silence, not a louder file.

deal.landM4 · S6b mount · 9 backs seat 90 ms apart, 420 ms in the air≤80 ms · −18 dBFS · RR3

A card landing flat on felt. Same 90 ms density problem as the reveal, same cure — under 80 ms after trim, three variants cycled. Try flip.worn first before generating: same material, similar action. deal.throw — the flick off his fist, ≤70 ms, −24 — is optional and probably too much: from t=420 throws and lands interleave 90 ms apart. Build land, test, add throw only if the deal feels weightless without it.

reveal.arriveM14 · S8 mount · the earned set set down face-down≤280 ms · −14 dBFS · 1 file

One soft stack placed on the table. Must finish inside LEAD_MS (300) or it eats the first flip. The run was just written to disk in this instant — the loudest moment in the app is currently a screen swap; this is its sound until M14 gives it a picture. Schedule it at t=0 in the same audio-clock pass as the flips.

TIER 3Chrome — by category, never by button8 cues · 6 files
ui.nav← back links · deck rows · set rows · VIEW COLLECTION → · CREDITS · BACK TO DECK≤50 ms · −20 dBFS · RR2 ±3%

One dry cardstock tap, 300–800 Hz. The cursor sound. It is also the screen transition — there is no separate transition cue. ui.select (a deck row going live on S1, CTA turns strike) is ui.nav at rate 1.12: a state change sits a shade above navigation, no new file.

ui.primarySTART SESSION · KEEP WRITING · the bone and strike CTAs without a cue of their own≤120 ms · −14 dBFS · 1 file

Felt-covered wood pressed, heavier than nav. The confirm. DEAL and FLIP are excluded — they have deal.press and ink.reveal, because what they confirm is a specific object.

ui.toggleDAKUTEN on/off · SOUND on/off≤60 ms · −16 dBFS · 1 file, 2 rates

A small wooden switch. ON at rate 1.0, OFF at 0.89. The sound toggle switching ON is the one cue that must always play — it is how the player learns the channel exists at all. Fire it inside the same gesture that unlocks the AudioContext.

modal.open · modal.closeM8 · the abandon dialog · ✕ / Esc / backdrop / KEEP WRITING≤160 · ≤100 ms · −16 · −18 dBFS · 2 files

Open: a felt-muffled thump as the blur comes in. Close: lighter and shorter — the cancel. LEAVE RUN reuses reveal.skip at −18: cards gathered up all at once is exactly what happens to the hand, and it answers OVL-5 without adding a word the modal already said.

overlay.lift · overlay.setOVL-1 · tap a card on S8 after the reveal · tap anywhere to close≤120 ms each · −18 dBFS · 2 files

One card lifted off the table, then set back down. Do not reverse the lift for the set — reversed foley reads as reversed. Two short generations.

TIER 4Deliberately silent — do not build
The count pop on S8 (M11) — the flip already is its sound. The shiny sweep — flip.shiny's ring is the sweep. The float and bob of seated cards on S6b. Panel entrance, S1 hydration, the 120 ms type-on lead-in. Screen transitions — ui.nav is the transition. Error or success chimes — nothing in this app is an error. Pronunciation, mora audio — backlog, not SFX (§5.4). Music. The pencil loop is the score.

FLAGSSix things the screen review turned up that the spec does not yet answer

01 · THE GOT IT WINDOW
t=0 the melt starts and the earned.* line begins typing (120 ms lead-in); the player may already be drawing; t=420 the tick lands. Four sounds in half a second, every single word. Rule: melt and talk stay at −24, ink.loop ducks talk by 6 dB while active, the tick is never ducked.

02 · HE TALKS ON EVERY PROMPT
An earned.* or round.* line types on for every word. After the pencil, joker.talk becomes the most repeated sound in the app. Decide: does the type-on click for every line, or only for the first line of a screen, a miss, and a once-line — with round.* lines typing silently? I would start with the second.

03 · THE TOGGLE HAS NO HOME
There is no settings screen. Candidates: a SOUND row beside CREDITS at the foot of S1 in the same legend style, or a line in the ✕ dialog. The S1 foot is the honest place — it is where the app already puts the things that are not the game.

04 · audioSession TYPE
§4 says 'transient', which fits one-shots. A 2 s pencil loop under 'transient' is a stretch; 'ambient' also respects the ringer switch and mixes with other audio. Check both on device with the loop running — the ringer-switch behaviour is the law, the label is not.

05 · REDUCED MOTION
The melt becomes a 160 ms fade → no prompt.melt. The deal becomes a 120 ms fade → one deal.land, not nine. The hand tick loses its ghost → the tick still plays, at the moment the count updates. S8 → reveal.end only, as specced.

06 · THE BUDGET
21 new generations at 0.5 s (two at 1 s, one at 2 s for the loop) plus the specced seven. ~30 generations, 4 variants each. Ship weight stays under 200 KB at 96 kbps mono, the loop being the only file over 10 KB. Licensing check (§5.2) covers all of it in one pass.

Next: the §2-format prompts for Tiers 1–3, 450 characters each, anechoic clause on every one. The loop and joker.talk are the two that will take iteration — everything else is the same cardstock family the seven already belong to.
